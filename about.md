# About TrueScoped

TrueScoped is a free, useful information website. Every page answers one
real problem that ordinary internet users actually search for. We do not
write generic blog posts. We do not chase news. We do not run pop-ups or
intrusive ads.

## What we do

1. We discover real problems people Google — using live web research across
   Reddit, official support communities (Apple, Google, Microsoft, WhatsApp,
   Netflix), and YouTube tutorials. The full research methodology is
   documented in [`/RESEARCH.md`](/RESEARCH.md).
2. We write genuinely useful answers — short, plain-English, step-by-step.
3. We pair every procedural answer with a free in-browser tool when the
   problem is procedural (compress PDF, convert HEIC, etc.). The tool lives
   on the same page as the article — no redirect to a different site.
4. We host it on GitHub Pages for free. No paywall, no signup.

## How we make money

Three ad units are placed on every page:

- A 728x90 leaderboard at the top (so every visitor sees at least one ad).
- A 300x250 medium rectangle either in-content or below a tool result.
- A 468x60 banner above the footer.

We are intentionally light on ads. Three units per page is the upper bound.
Too many ads hurt ranking, hurt UX, and don't earn much more.

## Who we are not

We are not an SEO content mill. We do not produce 5,000 pages of generic
content. We do not keyword-stuff. We do not buy backlinks. The 100-article
roadmap in [`/ROADMAP.md`](/ROADMAP.md) is the entire first-year plan.
