# 100-Article Roadmap — TrueScoped (Files Niche)

> 100 articles organized in 10 clusters of 10. Each cluster has one pillar article, supporting tutorials, one troubleshooting article, and one FAQ article. Internal-link map below.

## Internal-linking plan

- Cluster 1 (PDF Compression) pillar links to: every size-specific tutorial (1MB, 300KB, 100KB, 50KB) AND to Cluster 9 'Send large PDF by email'.
- Cluster 2 (PDF Editing) pillar links to: every page-operation tutorial AND to Cluster 3 (PDF Conversion).
- Cluster 3 (PDF Conversion) pillar links to: every format-pair tutorial AND back to Cluster 2 (Editing).
- Cluster 4 (HEIC) pillar links to: every device-specific tutorial AND to Cluster 3 'Convert JPG to PDF' AND to Cluster 5 'Compress an image'.
- Cluster 5 (Image Compress) pillar links to: every size-specific tutorial AND to Cluster 6 'Remove background'.
- Cluster 6 (Background Remover) pillar links to: every background tutorial AND to Cluster 5 'Compress an image'.
- Cluster 7 (OCR) pillar links to: every device-specific OCR tutorial AND to Cluster 3 'Make scanned PDF searchable'.
- Cluster 8 (Video Compress) pillar links to: every video tutorial AND to Cluster 10 'MP4 to MP3'.
- Cluster 9 (Send Large Files) pillar links to: every send tutorial AND back to Cluster 1 'Compress PDF for email' AND Cluster 8 'Compress video for email'.
- Cluster 10 (Format Conversions) pillar links to: every individual format tutorial AND to Cluster 4 'HEIC conversion'.

## Article taxonomy

- **Pillar** — comprehensive guide for a cluster (~1,800–2,500 words). Optimized for the head term.
- **Supporting** — conceptual explainer (~800–1,200 words). Fills gaps the pillar glosses over.
- **Tutorial** — step-by-step how-to (~600–1,000 words). Pairs with a free on-page tool when relevant. The long-tail traffic workhorse.
- **Troubleshooting** — moment-of-failure queries (~500–800 words). High urgency, high SERP CTR.
- **Comparison** — A vs B articles (~800–1,200 words). Naturally monetizable later with affiliate links.
- **FAQ** — short, snippet-friendly (~200–400 words). Targets Google PAA boxes and voice search.
