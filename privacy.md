# Privacy Policy

TrueScoped is a free, ad-supported information site hosted on GitHub Pages.
This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

## What we collect

- **Ad networks** may set cookies and track your behavior across pages on
  this site and across other sites. The ad networks currently used by
  TrueScoped are listed in `/ads/README.md` and in `_data/ads.yml`. The
  network in use as of launch is **versatilesentiment.com**.
- **Web analytics** may be enabled in the future. If enabled, this page will
  be updated to disclose which provider is used.
- **Tool usage**: the tools on this site are 100% client-side. Files you
  upload to a tool (a PDF you want to compress, an image you want to convert)
  never leave your browser. We do not see them, do not store them, do not
  transmit them anywhere.

## What we do not collect

- We do not require accounts. We do not collect names or emails.
- We do not sell your data. We have nothing to sell.

## Your choices

- **Disable cookies** in your browser settings.
- **Enable Do Not Track** in your browser. The `ads/ads.js` loader respects
  `navigator.doNotTrack === '1'` and will not inject any ad unit when set.
